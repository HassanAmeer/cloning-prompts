Deterministic logit classification

Unlike traditional LLMs (GPT-4, Claude) which suffer from 1-4 second token-by-token generation latency and potential generative hallucinations, Jev evaluates context and decision conditions in a single non-autoregressive forward pass.

All decisions, classifications, and probability distributions are calculated directly from representation logits, ensuring 100% deterministic outputs with zero hallucination.
