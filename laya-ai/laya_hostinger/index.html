Supported Decision Types

                

                    
1. noul / boolean (Boolean Probability): Calibrated Yes/No probability for binary statements. Returns summary.verdict ("Yes / True" or "No / False"), probability (0.0 – 1.0) and confidence score.

                    
2. choice (Multiple Choice): Selects the best match from custom comma-separated options (e.g. option_a, option_b, option_c) with full probability distribution.

                    
3. classification (Routing & Categorization): Directs tickets, leads, or content into categories (e.g. billing, tech_support, sales, account_security).

                    
4. score (Ordinal Scoring & Severity Rubric): Assigns a continuous calibrated rating across ordered rubric tiers (e.g. very low, low, medium, high, critical or numeric scale).

                    
5. sentiment (Sentiment Analysis): Classifies customer sentiment instantaneously (positive, neutral, negative) with zero text generation delay.

                    
6. risk (Risk & Urgency Assessment): Evaluates operational or security threat level (critical, high, medium, low) for instant dispatch.

                    
7. intent (Intent Classification): Maps user input to actionable intents (inquiry, complaint, upgrade, cancellation, feedback).
