📊 VPS RAM & Context Sizing Matrix
How context window selection impacts CPU RAM usage, speed, and accuracy on this Hostinger VPS:
RAM Tier Recommended Context RAM Footprint Accuracy & Capabilities Hostinger VPS Status
1GB – 2GB 2,048 tokens (2.05k) ~450 MB Short single-sentence intent classification. Low Spec
2GB – 4GB 4,096 tokens (4.10k) ~850 MB Standard customer support inquiries and emails. Adequate
4GB – 8GB 8,192 tokens (8.19k) ~1.4 GB Full multi-paragraph documents, 100+ languages, highest accuracy. Optimal for This VPS
16GB+ 16,384 tokens (16.38k) ~2.8 GB Long contracts, legal documents, large batching. Enterprise
Why 8.19k tokens is optimal for your 8GB VPS:
Laya evaluates the entire context in a single forward pass without token autoregression. Setting the context window to 8.19k tokens requires ~1.4GB of RAM, leaving ~6.6GB of free RAM for the Linux kernel, mail services (postfix/dovecot/roundcube), and background tasks. It delivers sub-second (~500ms) decision inference on 2 vCPU with 0% hallucinations.
